# Employment Contract Templates

**DISCLAIMER: These templates are for informational purposes only and do not constitute legal advice. Consult qualified legal counsel before using any employment documents.**

## Workflow

1. **Identify document type** -- determine which template is needed (offer letter, employment agreement, handbook section)
2. **Select jurisdiction** -- confirm at-will vs. contract state, applicable labor laws
3. **Customize template** -- fill in all bracketed placeholders with company-specific details
4. **Add required clauses** -- include jurisdiction-specific requirements (at-will language, non-compete enforceability)
5. **Legal review** -- have qualified counsel review before use
6. **Validate completeness** -- confirm all placeholders filled, signatures lines present, required exhibits attached

## Document Types

| Document | Purpose | When Used |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Offer Letter** | Initial job offer with key terms | Pre-hire |
| **Employment Contract** | Formal binding agreement | Hire |
| **Employee Handbook** | Policies and procedures | Onboarding |
| **NDA** | Confidentiality protection | Before access to proprietary info |
| **Non-Compete** | Competition restriction | Hire or Exit |

## Legal Framework

Key classification decisions that affect template selection:
- **At-Will vs. Contract** -- determines termination provisions
- **Employee vs. Contractor** -- determines tax, benefits, IP treatment
- **Exempt vs. Non-Exempt** -- determines overtime and hour tracking
- **Jurisdiction** -- state/country-specific requirements vary significantly

## Template 1: Offer Letter

Key sections to include:
- Position details (title, department, reports-to, location, start date, employment type)
- Compensation (base salary, bonus structure, equity if applicable)
- Benefits summary (health, retirement, PTO)
- Contingencies (background check, work authorization, required documents)
- At-will language (if applicable)
- Acceptance deadline and signature block

See `assets/offer_letter_template.md` for full template with placeholders.

## Template 2: Employment Agreement

Required sections:
1. **Employment** -- position, duties, best efforts, location
2. **Term** -- at-will or fixed term with start/end dates
3. **Compensation** -- salary, bonus, equity, benefits, expenses
4. **Confidentiality** -- definition of confidential information, non-disclosure obligations, return of materials, survival period
5. **Intellectual Property** -- work product assignment, prior inventions exhibit
6. **Non-Competition/Non-Solicitation** -- scope, duration, geographic area (enforceability varies by jurisdiction)
7. **Termination** -- for cause definition, without cause notice period, severance terms, surviving sections
8. **General Provisions** -- entire agreement, amendments, governing law, dispute resolution, severability, assignment, waiver
9. **Acknowledgments and Signatures**

See `assets/employment_agreement_template.md` for full template.

## Template 3: Employee Handbook Policies

Core policy sections:
- **Equal Employment Opportunity** -- protected characteristics, applicable practices
- **Anti-Harassment** -- prohibited conduct, reporting procedure, investigation process, retaliation protection
- **Work Hours** -- standard hours, core hours, flexible work, attendance expectations
- **PTO/Sick Leave** -- accrual rates by tenure, request procedures, maximum accrual
- **Code of Conduct** -- integrity, respect, confidentiality, conflict of interest, compliance
- **Technology/Security** -- acceptable use, monitoring disclosure, password/2FA requirements
- **Social Media** -- personal vs. company accounts, confidentiality obligations

See `assets/handbook_policy_template.md` for full template with acknowledgment form.

## Best Practices

**Do:**
- Consult legal counsel for each jurisdiction
- Keep signed copies of all agreements
- Update templates regularly as laws change
- Be clear and specific -- avoid ambiguity
- Train managers on policies and procedures

**Don't:**
- Use generic templates without jurisdiction customization
- Make promises that could create implied contracts
- Forget at-will language where applicable
- Skip legal review before deployment
